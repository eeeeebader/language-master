# Legal

German drafts (Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung, AGB) live in `apps/site/src/content` and in the
app; the admin publishes versioned texts and can require re-consent. Points covered: 12-month
content deletion, 24-month account deletion, no availability guarantee, minimum age 16,
Widerruf for digital content (§ 356 Abs. 5 BGB), third-country transfer to LLM providers.
Not legal advice; a lawyer reviews before launch.
